# Reading companions

The optional reading companions use non-chibi promotional character artwork:

- Okabe Rintaro and Makise Kurisu: https://steinsgate.jp/elite/
  (`common/img/character1.png` and `common/img/character2.png`).
- Iroha, Kaguya, and Yachiyo: https://www.cho-kaguyahime.com/
  (`assets/img/character/character_1main.webp`, `character_0main.webp`,
  and `character_2main.webp`).

Artwork belongs to its respective rights holders; it is not original project
artwork and no ownership or blanket redistribution license is claimed here.
The original files are embedded as data URLs in the `reader-companion-art`
JSON element in index.html for local, offline display. SVG view boxes frame
the characters, and CSS provides subtle swaying motion, not frame-by-frame
blinking or speaking animation. No third-party executable is needed.

Individual visibility checkboxes appear above the portraits. Changing the
visible cast clears manually dragged positions and arranges the selected
characters in equal-width slots on a shared baseline. Dragging remains
available until the next selection change; Reset positions restores the row.
Selections, size, pause state, and positions are remembered in this browser.
